On-device Network Monitoring

Privacy Policy

App Name: Internet Speed Meter & Ping

Last Updated: November 24, 2026

1. Overview

Internet Speed Meter & Ping is a network utility that displays transfer speed, latency, data usage, and per-app traffic.

The App processes network statistics on your device. We do not operate app accounts or a server that receives your traffic history. Network tests use external diagnostic providers. Versions with monetization also use Google services for ads and purchases and offer optional Microsoft Clarity usage analytics as described below.

2. Information Handled by the App

A. Network and traffic statistics

The App reads Android network counters and usage statistics to display Wi-Fi and mobile data consumption. It can display connection type, cellular network technology (such as 4G or 5G), signal strength, local IP address, and Wi-Fi network name where Android makes them available. Usage history, settings, data-plan configuration, and successful speed-test results are stored in the App's private storage.

The App does not intercept the content of other apps' network traffic, browsing history, passwords, or messages.

B. Installed apps and per-app data usage

When you grant Usage Access in Android settings, the App reads per-app network statistics. It accesses installed-app metadata, including package names, app names, icons, and Android user IDs, to label traffic statistics. On Android 11 and later, package visibility is limited to launchable apps and packages Android makes visible automatically. Traffic entries whose app metadata is unavailable may display a generic label or user ID. This information is processed on your device and is not uploaded to the developer or sent to the diagnostic providers. You can revoke Usage Access in Android settings.

C. Ping and latency measurements

Status-bar monitoring and user-initiated latency tests connect to the selected public endpoints, including Cloudflare, Google Public DNS, Quad9, or OpenDNS. These tests use TCP connections or ICMP requests to measure connection response times. They do not send your traffic history, installed-app list, or personal files.

The receiving provider necessarily sees the source public IP address and connection metadata. Its handling of those requests is governed by its own policies.

D. Speed tests

When you start a speed test, the App makes HTTPS requests to speed.cloudflare.com, downloads test data, and uploads generated test bytes. The uploaded payload is synthetic data, not your files or the contents of traffic from other apps. Cloudflare receives your public IP address and request metadata as part of these connections. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

Speed tests consume network data and can use a substantial amount on fast connections. Your mobile operator's normal data charges may apply. Completed test results are saved on your device; unsuccessful or cancelled tests are not saved as successful results.

E. Advertising

In versions with ads, the App may show Google AdMob banners on the dashboard, after completed speed and ping tests, in app traffic, and in traffic history. Occasional full-screen ads may also appear after completed speed or ping tests. App-open ads may appear during startup on the second launcher opening of the day, at most once per day. Pro removes all these ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect or share IP addresses, interactions with the app or ads, diagnostics, and device or account identifiers for advertising, measurement, and fraud prevention. The App does not include traffic history, speed-test values, or the installed-app list in ad requests. See Google's SDK data disclosure and Google's Privacy Policy.

The App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to update advertising privacy choices at launch and present a consent form where required, including for users in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland. When the Ad privacy options control is available in Settings, you can use it to review, change or withdraw your consent. The App clears loaded ads when you open this control and checks UMP's current permission before requesting new ads. Depending on your choices and the applicable requirements, Google may serve non-personalized or limited ads. The form explains the purposes and advertising partners involved.

F. Pro purchases

Google Play handles checkout and payment for Pro. Pro is available through an auto-renewing 3-month or yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase. The App checks purchase information from Google Play, such as the product ID, purchase status and token, to activate, acknowledge, and restore access. It stores purchase entitlement locally. Subscription access may be retained for up to 24 hours between successful Google Play checks, while a confirmed lifetime purchase has no subscription expiry. We do not receive your card details through the App.

G. Optional usage analytics (Microsoft Clarity)

Usage analytics is off by default. If you are at least 18 and choose to allow it in Settings, Microsoft Clarity may collect taps, scrolling, masked screen layouts, device specifications, app diagnostics and a random pseudonymous identifier to help us understand usability problems. The App masks its entire main view before initializing the SDK and never deliberately unmasks text or images. We do not supply a custom user identifier, personal files, traffic contents, installed-app metadata or purchase tokens to Clarity. Microsoft necessarily receives connection metadata such as the public IP address. See Microsoft's Privacy Statement and Clarity's Google Play data guidance.

After your opt-in, the App makes a small HTTPS request to Cloudflare to check the country associated with your current network IP address. This request does not include an app or user identifier, history or test results; the App uses only the country code and does not store or log the returned IP address. Recording remains off until a permitted country is verified. All European countries (including Ukraine, the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), listed European dependencies, transcontinental countries and additional restricted destinations are excluded. Unknown or failed lookups, detected VPNs/proxies, metered networks and contradictory blocked-region device signals do not permit recording. IP geolocation and device hints are approximate and cannot guarantee a person's physical location.

Recording is paused when the App loses focus or leaves the foreground, during speed and ping tests, when the active network changes, or when the region check expires. The SDK's independent background upload job is disabled. You can withdraw consent at any time by switching off Usage analytics in Settings; this stops future recording and revokes the SDK analytics signal. Your choice expires after 180 days and is excluded from Android backup and device transfer. This choice is separate from advertising consent, and the App never grants Microsoft advertising-storage consent. Withdrawing consent does not automatically delete information already received by Microsoft. For questions or data-deletion requests, contact us using the email below.

4. Third-Party Services and Support

Network diagnostic providers, Google and, after optional analytics consent in an eligible region, Microsoft receive the information described in section 2 for their respective features. We do not sell your usage records or installed-app information.

If you choose to share a result using Android's share menu, the content you select is sent to the recipient or app you choose and is subject to that app's policies.

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use that correspondence to respond to your request and troubleshoot the reported issue. Do not send passwords or other information unnecessary for support.

5. Storage, Backup, Retention, and Deletion

Settings, usage history, data-plan configuration, and speed-test history are stored in Android's private app storage. Android app backup is enabled in the current version. Depending on your device, Android version, and system settings, app data may be included in your system backup or transferred to another device. The backup provider controls its backup storage and deletion. We do not receive or operate these system backups.

Local records are retained until removed through the available history controls, clearing the App's storage in Android settings, or uninstalling the App. Clearing traffic history and speed-test history are separate actions; clearing traffic history may keep the current day's active counters. Uninstalling removes local app data, but does not necessarily remove system backups or support correspondence. A system restore may restore previously backed-up app data.

You can stop monitoring and disable optional features in the App, and revoke Usage Access, overlay access, notifications, and battery exemptions through Android settings. For questions about support correspondence or deletion of information sent to us, email the contact below.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us through support, contact us so we can address the request.
